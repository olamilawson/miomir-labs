Work

What we do

We design, build, and maintain systems that hold important state.

Records and ledgers

Transaction logs, registries, and audit trails. We build software that keeps an accurate history and makes it searchable.

Identity and access

Authentication, authorization, and credential flows for staff, customers, and devices.

Settlement systems

Payment reconciliation, reporting, and the glue between banks, processors, and internal accounts.

Applied research

Document extraction, classification, and retrieval systems for organizations with large paper or PDF backlogs.

How we work

We start by reading your existing code and talking to the people who operate it. We prototype against real data, not slide decks. We hand over systems we would be happy to maintain ourselves.

Most of our engagements are long. We prefer to understand a domain deeply rather than ship fast and leave.
