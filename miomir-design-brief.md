# Miomir Labs — Website Design Brief

## The company

Miomir Labs is an engineering and research house in Lagos. It builds systems of record for clients, runs a small number of its own software products, and does applied research on AI in production.

The domain is ledgers, identity, records and money movement. The layer underneath a business, where errors compound and outages are visible.

The name is Slavic. *Mio* means dear, *mir* means peace. The reading the brand takes: the measure of infrastructure is how rarely anyone has to think about it.

## The job of the site

One page, plus a contact route. Launching mission-first, because the portfolio is mid-restructure and case studies are weeks away.

A visitor should decide within about ten seconds that these people are serious, and within a minute that they are unusually disciplined.

## Audience

Two, in this order.

Someone at a bank, a funded company or a marketplace who is deciding whether to trust this house with infrastructure. Older, sceptical, has been burned by a vendor before.

An engineer or researcher considering joining. Reads the site for evidence of taste.

Both want proof and neither wants persuasion.

## Tone

Sober. Confident without volume. The register of a firm that turns work down.

Copy will be short. Assume every block is a third the length of a typical marketing site. Design for sparseness rather than accommodating it.

## Art direction

**Discipline over decoration.** Minimal, precise, generous with space. Elegance comes from spacing, type and restraint, not from effects.

**Starting palette,** open to argument if the alternative is better reasoned. Deep oxblood (#1E0C11 to #421A23) as the ground, bone (#EFE8DC) for type, brass (#C89A4A) for rules and single accents, a muted sage (#8AA48F) for state indicators. Oxblood references bound ledgers and puts distance between this and every navy technology site.

**Starting type,** same latitude. A characterful grotesque for display used sparingly and large, a serif for reading text, a monospace for anything that represents system data. Make the pairing itself part of the identity.

**The signature.** The page carries a double-entry device: two columns running against each other, one stating what the house commits to and the other what that commitment costs it. This is the memorable element and the reason to visit. Everything else stays quiet around it. Treat it as a ledger rather than a comparison table.

**Motion.** One orchestrated moment at most, on the signature element. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Scattered hover effects will read as an AI-generated site.

## Structure

Sticky minimal header.

Hero: a single statement, a short supporting line, and a small etymology note.

The double-entry ledger: four or five paired lines.

A short essay section on why the house exists. This is the one place with sustained prose, and it should break the visual register, likely by inverting to a light ground.

What the house does: three areas, equal weight, no numbering.

Current status: a plain list of work in progress with states, no client names.

Contact and hiring.

## Constraints

No stock photography. No people in golden light. No abstract data visualisations. No gradient meshes.

No logo carousel. No client names anywhere until cleared.

No numbered 01/02/03 feature cards unless the content is genuinely a sequence.

No claims about innovation, clarity, excellence or transformation.

Avoid the three current defaults in AI-generated design: cream ground with high-contrast serif and terracotta accent; near-black with a single acid accent; broadsheet hairline grid with dense columns.

## Technical

Single static page. No framework required. Fast on a Nigerian mobile connection, which means self-hosted or subset fonts and no heavy assets.

Responsive to 360px. Visible keyboard focus. Semantic headings. Reduced motion honoured. Contrast passing AA on every text and background pair.

## Deliverables

Desktop and mobile design for the page. The type scale, palette and spacing system as tokens. A note on the signature element's behaviour and any motion.

## Reference

A working HTML prototype exists and can be shared. Treat it as an argument about structure and direction, not as a layout to reproduce. Its copy is heavier than the final site will carry.

## Open

The brand mark and wordmark are unresolved. The domain is unconfirmed. Neither blocks the page design.
