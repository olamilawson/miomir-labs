01 / Practice Areas

What we do

We build and maintain the systems that hold your records and move your money.

Records

Records & ledgers

Transaction logs, registries, and audit trails. We build the history your auditors read and your lawyers reach for when a customer disputes a charge, so a balance you query today matches the one you queried last quarter.

Audit logs · Data registries · Double-entry ledgers
Identity

Identity & access

Authentication, granular authorization, and credential lifecycles. We write the rules for who may touch what, then keep them readable by your compliance team without a translator.

RBAC / ABAC · Credential issuance · SSO & mTLS
Settlement

Settlement systems

Reconciliation, financial reporting pipelines, and the middleware between commercial banks, processors, and your core ledger. We handle the duplicate postings and the half-finished transfers your team chases by hand today.

Reconciliation engines · Webhook gateways · Clearing pipelines
Research

Applied research

Document extraction, classification, and retrieval for institutions sitting on physical archives or decades of scanned PDFs. We measure what the model gets wrong before we let it near your workflow.

Document parsing · Knowledge retrieval · Optical classification

03 / Enterprise

For organizations

We embed with enterprise and government engineering teams to build infrastructure that outlasts the people who specified it.

Core Principles

Start with what you have

We audit your databases, your APIs, and the spreadsheets your team relies on before we propose anything new. The bottleneck sits between your systems more often than inside any one of them.

Build for your environment

We design for the latency, the dropped connections, and the server budget you have in West Africa.

Own the outcome

You keep the source, the documentation, and a handover plan your own engineers can act on. We do not hold your system hostage.

How an engagement runs

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We interview your operators, read your source, and map how data moves between teams.

  2. 02

    Design

    We specify a system simple enough that your team can hold it in their heads, and write down the invariants it has to hold.

  3. 03

    Build

    We ship production increments, each one backed by tests and an audit trail you can inspect.

  4. 04

    Handover

    We train your engineers, transfer the repository, and agree what support looks like afterwards.

04 / Engineering Philosophy

Build for the constraint

Systems here run under high latency, modest infrastructure budgets, and close regulatory attention. We design for those conditions at the start, because retrofitting them costs more than planning for them.

We write code a stranger can audit. That stranger might be your next hire, your regulator, or one of us in three years with no memory of the decision.

Generating code is now the cheap part of this work. We spend our hours on the expensive part, deciding whether the code is right and proving it stays right as the system changes.

When we do applied research, we test against your real transactions and the feedback of the people who process them, rather than a benchmark that flatters the model.
