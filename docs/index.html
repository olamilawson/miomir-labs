How we work

Deep domain engagement

We start by reading your existing codebase and interviewing the operators who run it every day. We prototype directly against production data constraints, not conceptual slide decks.

Most of our client engagements are long-term. We prefer to understand a domain deeply and ship systems we would be proud to maintain ourselves.

02 / Enterprise

For organizations

We embed with enterprise engineering and government teams to build infrastructure that outlasts the people who originally specified it.

Core Principles

Start with what you have

We audit existing databases, APIs, and manual workarounds before proposing new components. Most enterprise bottlenecks are integration problems.

Build for your environment

We architect for network latency, intermittent connectivity, modest server specs, and the strict regulatory realities of West Africa.

Own the outcome

You receive full source code, comprehensive documentation, and a clear operational handover plan. No vendor lock-in.

03 / Engineering Philosophy

Build for the constraint

Most software systems in emerging markets must operate reliably under high latency, modest infrastructure budgets, and intense regulatory scrutiny. We design for those hard conditions first rather than patching them later.

We write clean, readable code that is straightforward to audit, repair, and maintain for years without operational friction. When conducting applied research, we stay grounded in actual transaction data and direct operator feedback.
