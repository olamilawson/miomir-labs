01 / Practice Areas

What we do

We build and maintain the systems that hold your records and move your money, along with the screens your operators use to run them.

Records

Records & ledgers

Transaction logs, registries, and audit trails. We build the history your auditors read and your lawyers reach for when a customer disputes a charge, so a balance you query today matches the one you queried last quarter.

Audit logs · Data registries · Double-entry ledgers
Identity

Identity & access

Authentication, granular authorization, and credential lifecycles. We write the rules for who may touch what, then keep them readable by your compliance team without a translator.

RBAC / ABAC · KYB against CAC and BVN · SSO & mTLS
Settlement

Settlement systems

Reconciliation, financial reporting pipelines, and the middleware between commercial banks, processors, and your core ledger. We handle the duplicate postings and the half-finished transfers your team chases by hand today.

Reconciliation engines · NIBSS and processor integrations · Clearing pipelines
Product

Operator-facing product

The screens a trader uses on a phone to invoice a customer, take a payment, and see what is left in stock. We design the interface and the ledger under it, for the device that is the only computer the owner has.

Native mobile · Offline-tolerant sync · Merchant onboarding
Research

Applied research

Document extraction, classification, and retrieval for institutions sitting on physical archives or decades of scanned PDFs. Where a score drives a decision, it ships with the documentation and audit trail a risk function needs to question it.

Document parsing · Knowledge retrieval · Explainable scoring · Model documentation

03 / Engineering Philosophy

Build for the constraint

We design for the connections, devices, and server budget your operators have. We agree how the system should behave through a dropped connection or a delayed response before choosing the architecture.

We map your consent, data retention, and access requirements at the start. For applied research, we evaluate against your records and the decisions your operators need to make.
